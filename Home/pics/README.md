# Homepage image placeholders

Add these manually before launch:

- `pc-tower.jpg`: a wide, high-contrast gaming PC tower photo for the hero, category card, and featured product imagery. Recommended: landscape JPG, 1600×1000 or larger.
- `parts-detail.jpg`: a close-up photo of PC components for the PC Parts category and hero imagery. Recommended: landscape JPG, 1200×900 or larger.
- `pc-tower-close.jpg`: a close-up gaming PC tower photo for alternate hero slides. Recommended: landscape JPG, 1600×1000 or larger.
