# Prebuilt PC image placeholders

Add these manually:

- `pc-tower.jpg`: product photo for entry and mid-range prebuilt system cards. Recommended: landscape JPG, 1200×900 or larger.
- `pc-tower-close.jpg`: close-up product photo for higher-end prebuilt cards and the page hero. Recommended: landscape JPG, 1600×1000 or larger.
